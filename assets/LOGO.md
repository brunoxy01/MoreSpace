> Use case: logo-brand. Create a single Windows application icon for '+Espaço', a disk-space management app. A clearly recognizable compact hard disk drive (HDD): rounded rectangular drive body, simple circular platter and short actuator arm, with one bold plus symbol integrated at the lower-right corner. Refined minimal flat icon, chunky simple geometry to read at small sizes. Deep emerald teal #216956 drive with white platter details, bright mint plus. No text, no letters, no multiple icons, no perspective scene, no desktop mockup, no exterior shadows. Centered square composition, icon fills approximately 85% of canvas; transparent background outside the drive and plus. Professional understated look consistent with a clean white-and-teal Windows utility.

Arquivos: `logo.png` (original) e `app.ico` (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 e 256 px).
